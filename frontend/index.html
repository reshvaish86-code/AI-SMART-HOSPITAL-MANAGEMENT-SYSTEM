Automated 1-Hour & Medication Reminders

When you book an appointment or receive a prescription in your portal, our automated IST background cron engine executes 4 synchronized communication channels.

1. Voice Speech Alarm

The browser reads aloud your consultation details, doctor's name, and clinic room exactly 1 hour before your scheduled time.

2. Web Audio Chime

Plays a gentle tri-tone acoustic chime in your browser window to alert you without disruptive noise.

3. Brevo Email Digest

Dispatches formatted HTML clinical tickets directly to your registered email with doctor notes, timings, and directions.

4. Browser Push Alert

Delivers interactive desktop notification pop-ups with 1-click access to your consultation queue.
